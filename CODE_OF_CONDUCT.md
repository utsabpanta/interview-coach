# Code of conduct

This project is for people preparing for job interviews, many of them under stress. Be the kind
of contributor you would want reviewing your own work on a bad day.

## Expected

- Be respectful and patient, especially with newcomers and people writing in a second language.
- Criticize code, questions, and ideas, not the people who wrote them.
- Assume good intent, and ask before assuming otherwise.
- Keep examples free of other people's private information: no real candidates' answers, no
  confidential interview material.

## Not acceptable

- Harassment, insults, or discrimination of any kind.
- Sexual language or imagery, or unwelcome attention.
- Publishing someone's private information.
- Content that helps people cheat in real interviews, such as tools to feed answers during one.

## If something goes wrong

Report it privately to the maintainer through the contact details on their GitHub profile.
Reports are kept confidential. The maintainer may remove comments, commits, or contributions, and
may block people who do not follow this code.

This code is adapted in spirit from the Contributor Covenant: https://www.contributor-covenant.org
